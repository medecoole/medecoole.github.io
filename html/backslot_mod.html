General Info

The Backslot Mod is a mod that allows you to wear items on your back to use as an extra hotbar slot.

With two Keybinds you can easly acces the slot without it being directly in your inventory.

By default you keep the item that is in the backslot post-death, but this can be changed using the config.

Keybinds

This mod adds 2 keybinds, wich are both configurable like any vanilla keybind.

Swap Item [G] swaps the held item with your current backslot item.

Select Item [X] selects the backslot item for using, dropping and attacking.
